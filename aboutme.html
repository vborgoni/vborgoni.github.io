A Little Bit About Me

Hi! My name is Vision Borgonia. As a 28-year-old artist of Mexican and Filipino descent, my work is a deeply personal exploration of identity,culture, and memory. My artistic foundation began early-and fiercly- leading my poor mother to scrub our living room walls before she eventually gave up and allowed me to design full-scale murals all over the wall of the house. This passion eventually led me to a performing arts high school, where i studied within the Production and Design conservatory.

My Inspiration

My creative vision is fueled by the rich traditions of my dual heritage, the strange logic of surrealism, the textures of everyday life, and my own personal memories and experiences. I am profoundly moved by artist who challenge boundaries and capture the human experience with vibrant colors, including:

  • The Surreal & Intimate: Frida Kahlo, Leonara Carrington, and Maria Fragoso
  • The Bold & Conceptual:Yayoi Kusama, Barbara Kruger, and Banksy
  • From My Everyday World:My Dad, whose influence guides my creativity, and the daily companionship of my two dogs and boyfriend
